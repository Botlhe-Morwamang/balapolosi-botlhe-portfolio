# Exercise 02: Portfolio Skeleton
### 35 minutes — the main guided practical

## Purpose

This is the start of your **continuous portfolio project** — the site you'll keep building throughout every mission. Today you're building the **skeleton only**: structure and placeholders. No styling (that's Mission 02), and no fully-written content (that's homework).

## Why "Skeleton Only" Matters

If you try to write your entire bio and three full project descriptions in 35 minutes, you'll run out of time before the structure is even right — and structure is what Mission 02's CSS depends on. Get the shape correct first. Content comes later.

## What You'll Build In Session

A single HTML page with these sections, in this order — **each one can just be a heading and a placeholder line or two, nothing more, for now**:

1. **Header** — your name/brand + a nav placeholder.
2. **Nav** — links to each section (Home, About, Skills, Projects, Contact).
3. **Hero** — your name, title, one sentence (you can copy this straight from Exercise 01).
4. **About** — just a heading and one placeholder sentence for now.
5. **Skills** — a heading and an empty or 2-3 item list.
6. **Projects** — a heading and 2-3 empty `<article>` blocks (titles can just say "Project 1", "Project 2" for now).
7. **Contact** — a heading and one placeholder line.
8. **Footer** — a short copyright line.

## Requirements Checklist (Session Scope Only)

- [ ] Uses `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<footer>` — not `<div>` for these roles.
- [ ] Uses `<section>` for each content block, each with an `id` attribute.
- [ ] Nav links use `<a href="#section-id">` and jump to the right section.
- [ ] Exactly one `<h1>` on the whole page (Hero section). Section headings use `<h2>`.
- [ ] All 8 sections exist — even if the content inside is just a placeholder sentence.

**Do not** worry yet about:
- Writing your real, full bio.
- Writing real project descriptions.
- Any colour, spacing, or layout — that's Mission 02.

## Where To Start

Open `starter/index.html`. The skeleton and `<!-- TODO -->` markers are there. Fill in placeholders, get the nav links working, and stop there for the session.

## How To Check Your Work (Quick Version)

1. Click every nav link — does it jump to the right section?
2. Right-click → Inspect — count your `<h1>` tags. Should be exactly one.

Save the full validator check and content polish for homework.

## After The Session

Everything else — real content, extra sections, the Final Challenge — is in `after-session.md` at the mission root. This exercise file's job ends at "skeleton exists and nav works."
